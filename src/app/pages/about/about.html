About East Lanka

East Lanka is a Sri Lankan product catalogue built around a simple idea: new products, better tomorrow.

We share finds from electronics, fashion, home, accessories and gifts — the same pieces you see on our Facebook and Instagram pages — then help you order through WhatsApp.

There is no self-checkout on this site yet. When you tap Order on WhatsApp, we receive a message with the product name, price and code. We confirm whether it is available, agree delivery, and look after the rest with you.

That keeps things personal while the catalogue stays easy to browse on a phone.
